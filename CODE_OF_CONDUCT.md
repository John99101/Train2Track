# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone who takes part in this project: in issues, pull requests,
reviews and every other place that belongs to it.

## What is expected

- Be kind, patient and factual. Criticise the code or the idea, not the person.
- Assume good intentions. Ask before you accuse.
- Accept a decision of the maintainer, also when you disagree.
- Write in English or German, so that others can follow.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, and remarks that put people down because of who they
  are.
- Publishing private information of others.
- Naming, picturing or describing ticket inspectors, staff of transport companies or
  riders.
- Calls or tips to travel without a ticket, to evade inspections or to break the law in
  any other way.
- Advertising and off topic content.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, and block people
from the repository, at his own judgement.

To report a problem in confidence, write to info.train2track@gmail.com.
